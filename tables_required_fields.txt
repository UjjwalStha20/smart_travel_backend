Table: Address (POST /addresses/)
  province   : required (str, min_length=1)
  district   : required (str, min_length=1)
  latitude   : required (float, ge=-90, le=90)
  longitude  : required (float, ge=-180, le=180)
  altitude   : required (int, gt=0)
  place      : optional (str | None)

Table: Destination (POST /destinations/)

  rating         : optional (int | None, ge=1, le=5)

  trekking_routes: optional (list[TrekkingRouteNested] | None)
